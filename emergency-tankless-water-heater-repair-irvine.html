No Hot Water, Shutdowns & Tankless Error Codes

Losing hot water changes the job from routine maintenance to an immediate household problem. Ultra Home Repair diagnoses the cause first, then explains whether the unit needs cleaning, a component repair, or a different solution.

Call when you have

  • No hot water
  • A tankless unit that will not ignite
  • An error code that remains or returns
  • A heater that repeatedly shuts down
  • Intermittent or suddenly reduced hot water

Service may include

  • System and error-code diagnosis
  • Ignition and flame-sensing checks
  • Venturi and combustion-air inspection
  • Cleaning or component replacement when appropriate
  • Flushing and descaling when scale is the actual problem

Tankless Water Heater Repair Questions

How much does tankless diagnosis and repair cost?

Repair pricing depends on the cause, required parts and access. Dustin confirms the repair scope and written estimate before work begins. The standard $150 tankless flush is a separate maintenance service.

Can I request an urgent repair appointment?

Call Dustin to check current availability. Business hours are daily, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM. Same-day or 24-hour service is not guaranteed.

Does no hot water always mean the unit needs replacement?

No. Shutdowns can be caused by maintenance conditions, ignition problems, airflow or venturi issues, sensors, wiring or failed components. Diagnosis should come before a replacement recommendation.

Can a flush restore a tankless heater that stopped working?

A flush may help when water-side scale is contributing to the problem. It will not correct unrelated ignition, venting, combustion, sensor, control-board or component faults, so diagnosis should come first when the unit has stopped working.

What if I smell gas or a carbon-monoxide alarm sounds?

Leave the area and contact the gas utility or emergency services from a safe location. Do not wait for a normal repair appointment.
