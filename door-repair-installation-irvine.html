Start with what’s happening

Door not latching, rubbing or out of alignment?

The symptom helps identify the next step. Moving the strike plate is only useful when it addresses the cause.

01

It closes, but won’t stay latched

The latch may be missing the strike opening, or the door may have shifted. Dustin checks the hinge fit, door position and latch engagement before recommending a strike adjustment.

02

It rubs, sticks or needs lifting

Uneven gaps and scraping can point to loose hinges or another alignment issue. The frame and door condition help determine whether adjustment or more involved work is appropriate.

03

The lock or handle feels wrong

A hard-to-turn deadbolt, loose handle or latch that does not retract can need hardware attention. Describe whether the problem happens with the door open, closed or both.

04

The door or trim needs replacing

If the existing frame is suitable, a slab can replace the door panel. A prehung unit replaces the door and frame together. Casing finishes the edges of the opening.

Choose the right starting point

A slab keeps the frame.
A prehung unit includes it.

Before buying a replacement, confirm the size, swing direction, thickness and hardware preparation. The right fit can save unnecessary changes to the opening.

A few useful answers

Door repair & installation questions

Why does my door close but not latch?

The latch may miss the strike opening because of loose or shifted hinges, uneven door alignment, a moved strike plate or a hardware problem. Dustin checks the fit before deciding whether to adjust the hinges, reposition the strike or recommend other work.

How much does it cost to fix a door that won’t latch?

The listed $95 service adjusts the small metal plate on the door frame so the latch or deadbolt lines up. It includes filling exposed gaps and old mounting holes, securing the plate, finishing the repaired area and touch-up painting with your supplied touch-up paint. Additional hinge, frame or hardware work is assessed separately.

What is the difference between a door slab and a prehung door?

A slab is the door panel. The $200 interior slab service fits a customer-supplied slab into a suitable existing frame using existing hinge and hardware locations. A prehung unit includes the door and its frame; interior installation labor is $160. Door units and materials are separate.

Does the $160 prehung price include trim?

Trim installation is a separate $150 labor service per opening, covering both sides. The door unit, trim and other materials are separate. Dustin confirms whether removal, finishing or other work is needed before scheduling.

Can you fix a door that sticks or rubs the frame?

Dustin can assess rubbing, sagging and alignment problems. The repair may involve hinges, the way the door sits in the frame or another fit issue. Alignment work beyond the listed strike plate adjustment is quoted for the actual scope.

What should I send for an estimate?

Tell Dustin your city, how many doors need attention and whether you need repair, a slab, a prehung unit or trim. A short video of the door closing and photos of the hinges, latch, strike plate and full opening are helpful. For installation, include the new product details if selected.
