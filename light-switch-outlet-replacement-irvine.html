Common questions

Planning your switch and outlet refresh.

Can you replace switches and outlets throughout an entire home?

Yes. Multi-room and whole-home cosmetic upgrades are welcome. We will review the device types, quantities, colors and wall plates before the project is scheduled.

Do I need to buy the switches and outlets?

Either option works. I can install devices you supply, or we can discuss providing standard replacement devices for the approved project.

Can you replace an existing electrical box?

Yes, an existing device box can be replaced when needed as part of the project. This service does not include installing new wiring.

Do you replace GFCI outlets?

Yes. Existing GFCI receptacles can be replaced along with standard outlets, switches, dimmers and matching wall plates.

Ready to modernize the details?

Tell me which rooms you want to refresh and roughly how many devices are involved.
