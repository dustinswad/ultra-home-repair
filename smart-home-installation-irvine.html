Product recommendations

Choose the ecosystem before choosing the device.

The best product is usually the one that works with the phones, voice assistants, subscriptions and other devices already in the home.

Good products to compare

  • Doorbells and cameras: Ring for a broad selection of wired, battery and light-equipped products; Google Nest for Google Home households.
  • Smart locks: Schlage Encode Plus for built-in Wi-Fi and Apple Home Key support.
  • Lighting: Lutron Caséta for hub-based whole-home control; Leviton Decora Smart for familiar paddle-style Wi-Fi controls.

Check before purchasing

  • HVAC compatibility and available thermostat terminals
  • Doorbell power requirements and transformer rating
  • Camera power source, mounting position and Wi-Fi strength
  • Door thickness, backset and smart-lock ecosystem
  • Switch load type, dimmable bulbs, multi-location control and any required hub
  • Optional subscription costs for recorded video or advanced features

Product details change. Confirm the current model and compatibility before ordering. Manufacturer references: Ring doorbells, Ring cameras, Nest doorbells, Nest cameras, Schlage Encode Plus, Lutron Caséta and Leviton Decora Smart.

Common questions

Before you order a smart device.

What smart home products can you install?

Customer-supplied video doorbells, security cameras, smart thermostats, smart locks, smart switches and dimmers. Basic app and Wi-Fi setup can be included.

Do you install both wired and battery video doorbells?

Yes. Dustin installs wired and battery products. Doorbell transformer replacement is also available when the selected wired product requires it.

Can you help me choose what to buy?

Yes. Send the product you are considering and what you want it to accomplish. Dustin can help compare compatibility and practical tradeoffs before you purchase it.

Who buys the equipment?

The customer purchases the products. That keeps the account, warranty, return rights and product selection in your name.

Can you configure the app?

Yes. Service can include pairing the device, updating it, naming it and setting up basic controls, notifications or activity zones. Account passwords stay with the customer.
