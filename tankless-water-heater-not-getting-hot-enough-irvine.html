Start With These Checks

A few observations can help distinguish a fixture problem, a capacity issue, overdue maintenance or a tankless-system fault.

Check the fixtures and demand

  • Test more than one faucet or shower
  • Confirm the temperature controller was not changed
  • Run one hot fixture by itself
  • Notice whether the problem appears only when several fixtures run

Check the heater's clues

  • Look for an error code on the display
  • Note whether the heater shuts down or keeps running
  • Consider when it was last flushed or descaled
  • Do not repeatedly clear a code that returns
If only one fixture is affected: the shower cartridge, mixing valve, aerator or showerhead may be the problem rather than the water heater.

When Scale May Be Contributing

Minerals can accumulate in the water side of a tankless heat exchanger. Depending on the unit and water conditions, scale may contribute to restricted flow, temperature fluctuation, noise or reduced heating performance.

What a flush does

A professional flush circulates an appropriate descaling solution through the water circuit, rinses the system and includes accessible inlet-filter cleaning. When scale is contributing to the symptom, maintenance may improve performance.

See Irvine tankless flush pricing and service details →

What a flush does not do

A water-side flush does not repair unrelated ignition, venting, combustion, sensor, control-board or failed-component problems. A displayed code or complete loss of hot water should be diagnosed rather than automatically treated as a maintenance issue.

When Diagnosis Is the Better Next Step

Schedule diagnosis when the symptoms point beyond routine maintenance.

Call when

  • The unit produces no hot water
  • An error code remains or returns
  • The heater repeatedly shuts down
  • The temperature problem began suddenly
  • A recent flush did not solve the problem

Service may include

  • Brand and model confirmation
  • Error-code and symptom diagnosis
  • Relevant water-flow and maintenance checks
  • Applicable vent and condensate inspection
  • An explanation before additional repair work
Safety: do not disassemble gas, combustion or sealed venting components as a homeowner troubleshooting step. If you smell gas or a carbon-monoxide alarm sounds, leave the area and contact the gas utility or emergency services from a safe location.

Not-Hot-Enough Questions

Will flushing make my tankless water heater hotter?

It may help when mineral scale is restricting water-side performance. If the cause is a fixture, capacity limitation, ignition fault, vent problem, sensor or component failure, a flush alone will not solve it.

What if only one shower is lukewarm?

When other fixtures produce properly hot water, the affected shower's cartridge, mixing valve or showerhead may be the more likely cause.

Do you diagnose the unit if a flush will not solve it?

Yes. Dustin provides tankless diagnosis and applicable repairs in addition to routine flushing.
