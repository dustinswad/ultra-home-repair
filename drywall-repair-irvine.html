Small holes to larger openings

More than covering the hole.

A good drywall repair starts with a supported patch and ends with a clear plan for texture and paint. Here’s the work Dustin can assess for your Irvine home.

01 / Wall repairs

Holes & access openings

Patch damaged wall sections and openings left after plumbing, electrical or other work. Install backing or support where needed, then fit the drywall and tape the seams.

02 / Ceiling repairs

Ceiling holes & patches

Repair local ceiling openings and damaged drywall sections, with patching and texture blending suited to the existing finish. Ceiling height, access and the condition of the surrounding area affect the estimate.

03 / Crack repairs

Cracks & damaged seams

Assess cracks and seam damage before agreeing on a repair. Share whether a crack has returned or the area has been wet; the underlying issue can affect whether a surface repair will hold.

04 / Finishing

Texture, primer & paint

Apply compound, sand and blend texture, then prime and paint the repair area as agreed. Texture, color and sheen can vary; a touch-up may remain visible even with carefully blended work.

Before you book

Drywall repair questions.

Can you patch a hole left after plumbing or electrical work?

Yes. Dustin can assess wall access openings and add backing or support where needed before installing and finishing the patch. Let him know whether the work behind the wall is complete before the opening is closed.

Can a small hole be repaired without replacing the whole wall?

Often, an opening can be patched locally. The size, surrounding drywall and available support determine the approach. Send a close photo and a wider view so Dustin can assess it.

Can you repair ceiling drywall and cracks too?

Yes. Dustin can assess local ceiling patches, openings, cracks and damaged drywall seams in Irvine. Include ceiling height, access and any history of leaks or recurring cracks when you send photos. The condition and cause of the damage may need attention before patching.

Will the texture and paint match exactly?

Texture and paint can be blended to the surrounding wall, but an exact match is not guaranteed. Existing texture, paint age, color, sheen and lighting can make a patch visible. A larger painted area may give a more uniform appearance; discuss the finishing scope before work starts.

Will my drywall repair take more than one visit?

It may. Compound, texture, primer and paint have different drying requirements. The repair method and conditions affect timing. Dustin will explain the expected stages and return visits for your repair.

Do you handle drywall repairs throughout Orange County?

The drywall repair service promoted on this page is focused on Irvine. Send your Irvine ZIP code with your photos when requesting an estimate.

Is sheetrock repair the same as drywall repair?

People commonly use “sheetrock repair” to mean drywall patching. Describe the damage or send photos; you don’t need to know the product name to request help.
