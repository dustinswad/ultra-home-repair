Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas does Ultra Home Repair serve?

Ultra Home Repair is based in Woodbury, Irvine and primarily serves Irvine neighborhoods including Woodbury, Great Park, Orchard Hills, Northwood, Turtle Rock, Quail Hill, and Portola Springs, plus Newport Beach, Corona del Mar, Laguna Beach, Dana Point, Laguna Niguel, Mission Viejo, Aliso Viejo, Lake Forest, Tustin, Costa Mesa, and the rest of Orange County.

What handyman services do you offer?

Complete handyman and home repair services including plumbing fixture replacement, electrical fixtures and outlets, drywall patching and paint touch-up, door and hardware repair, ceiling fan and light fixture installation, tankless water heater flushing, and general home maintenance and installation.

How fast can I get a quote?

Text or call (949) 371-6423, or submit a request through the contact form to request an estimate. Photos can help clarify the project.

Do you work with Realtors, landlords, and property managers?

Yes. Ultra Home Repair handles Realtor pre-listing repairs, post-inspection punch lists, tenant turnover work, and ongoing maintenance for landlords and property managers throughout Orange County.

How does pricing work?

You get a written estimate before any work starts, with a flat price — no hourly billing surprises. Because there's no general contractor markup, pricing stays transparent and competitive. A standard tankless water heater flush is $150, with an October promotional price of $125 for service completed by October 31, 2026.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, check, credit card, Zelle, and Venmo.
