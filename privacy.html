Privacy Policy

How Ultra Home Repair handles information submitted through this website.

Effective October 17, 2026

Notice at collection: If you submit the contact form, Ultra Home Repair collects the information you choose to provide so Dustin can respond to your request, prepare an estimate, and arrange service. This information is not sold or used for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Information you provide

The contact form asks for your first and last name, phone number, email address, service street address, city, state, ZIP code, and project description. If you call or text, you may choose to provide details, photographs, or a service address.

Information collected automatically

The website uses Google Analytics to understand visits and improve the site. Depending on your browser and settings, Analytics may collect page interactions, a device or browser identifier, browser and device information, and approximate geographic information. Ultra Home Repair does not ask Google Analytics to collect information entered into the contact form.

The website also uses temporary browser session storage to remember a broad referral source, such as Google, Nextdoor, or a tagged Google Business Profile link, and the page where that visit began. This information is included with an estimate request to help Ultra understand how people find the business. This referral record does not contain your name, contact details, full referring URL, or search terms. If browser storage is unavailable, you can still use the form.

How information is used

  • Respond to questions and service requests.
  • Prepare estimates, schedule work, and provide requested services.
  • Maintain business records and comply with legal obligations.
  • Protect the website from abuse and understand how the website performs.

Service providers

The contact form uses EmailJS to transmit your submission to Ultra Home Repair. The website is hosted through GitHub Pages and uses Google Analytics for website measurement. These providers process information for their respective services under their own terms and privacy practices.

Sale and sharing of personal information

Ultra Home Repair does not sell personal information and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to provide requested services, comply with law, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect legal rights.

Retention and security

Contact information and service records are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, business records, warranties, dispute resolution, and legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no website, email transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You do not have to use the website form; you may call or text instead. You may also control cookies through your browser or use Google's Analytics opt-out tools. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you previously submitted, call or text (949) 371-6423. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Children

This website and its services are intended for adults seeking home-repair services and are not directed to children under 13.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when website practices or service providers change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, call or text Ultra Home Repair at (949) 371-6423.
