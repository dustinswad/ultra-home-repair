Meet Dustin

Built on 17+ Years of Getting It Right

I'm Dustin — a longtime resident of Woodbury, Irvine, and the owner of Ultra Home Repair. I currently handle the work and customer communication myself. I started this business after years working in the construction industry, including running projects for a $60-million-a-year renovation firm, flipping houses, and personally managing dozens of full remodels.

What I learned from all of that: most homeowners don't need a general contractor. They need someone skilled and honest who communicates clearly, charges a fair price, and actually finishes the job.

That's what I built Ultra Home Repair to be: a locally owned and operated business with clear communication, careful work, and an agreed scope before work begins.

Why Ultra Home Repair

There's a Gap. I Built a Business to Fill It.

Most repairs are too small for a general contractor — but too important to ignore. Especially when a closing date or new tenant is on the line.

Smaller home-repair jobs are easy to put off when it is hard to find someone willing to handle them carefully.

Ultra Home Repair bridges that gap. I keep things lean and local — which means you get high-quality work, at honest prices, from someone who actually lives in this community and has a reputation to protect.

Whether you're a homeowner with a repair list, a Realtor with inspection fixes, or a property manager who needs a reliable go-to — I make the process simple and stress-free.
